Assistant Professor of Mathematics · International Coordinator, Fergana State Technical University

I am an applied mathematician based in Fergana, Uzbekistan. I build probabilistic and simulation models, especially Monte Carlo methods, for questions in groundwater, urban planning, transport and mathematical biology. I teach probability and statistics, linear algebra, higher mathematics and discrete structures. I also coordinate FSTU's international partnerships and Erasmus+ mobility.

Research

I work mainly with models and simulation rather than field data collection. Most of my projects use stochastic methods on problems from the Fergana Valley.

Stochastic groundwater modelling

Kriging, Monte Carlo ensembles and transient flow models on a Fergana Valley monitoring dataset of about 2,300 wells from 2015–2023.

Probabilistic urban suitability

Chained Monte Carlo frameworks that produce probabilistic construction-suitability zoning maps for Fergana City.

Mathematical biology

Geometric and probabilistic models of orb-web architecture and prey-capture efficiency.

Transport and fair fares

Monte Carlo indices of transit smoothness, mode-choice dynamics and how users respond to public transport prices.

Information theory of Uzbek

Entropy, redundancy and optimal coding in Uzbek-language texts.

Demography

Sex ratio at birth in Uzbekistan and forecasts of the age gap between men and women at first marriage.
